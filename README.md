Interactive Staff Attendance & Duty Roster Planner (កាលវិភាគការងារ និងវត្តមានបុគ្គលិក)
Target Users: Department heads, shift supervisors, hospital floor managers, and hospitality team leads.
Core Problem: Building fair monthly shift rosters across rotating teams and balancing leave requests is tedious on desktop spreadsheets and nearly impossible on small mobile phones.
Key Tablet Features:
Drag-and-Drop Monthly Grid: A calendar grid showing morning, afternoon, and night shifts where managers can reassign staff with a finger drag or stylus tap.
Leave & Shift Balance Counters: Real-time counters showing total duty hours per person to ensure work is shared equitably.
Direct Telegram Export: Exports the finalized monthly roster directly to group chats formatted as a high-resolution PNG or PDF.
Tablet Advantage: Displaying a 30-day timeline across 15+ staff members simultaneously requires the width of a landscape tablet display.
